# Bathrooms and wet zones

Normative source of zone definitions: ДСТУ HD 60364-7-701:2022 (and, in
older references, ДСТУ ГОСТ 30331.11:2004 — both are listed in ДБН
В.2.5-23:2025 normative references; if their texts conflict, surface the
conflict). ДБН В.2.5-23:2025 розділ 7 excerpts may add dwelling-specific
provisions — check `references/local/dbn-v2.5-23-2025/07-08-*.md` first.

**Zone boundaries (0/1/2), permitted equipment per zone, IP ratings, and
socket placement distances are numeric normative facts. Quote them from the
local excerpt or mark UNRESOLVED.** Do not reproduce remembered IEC zone
dimensions as Ukrainian requirements.

Checklist per wet room:

- [ ] Zone map of the room (needs: fixture positions and dimensions — never assumed)
- [ ] Each electrical item assigned a zone; permitted there per verified text
- [ ] IP rating per zone per verified text
- [ ] Residual protection of all bathroom circuits (parameters per verified text; else ENGINEERING RECOMMENDATION 30 mA + UNRESOLVED normative check)
- [ ] Fixed appliances (towel warmer, underfloor heating, boiler, washing machine): connection type, manufacturer requirements from real documentation
- [ ] Socket placement relative to zones
- [ ] Supplementary protective bonding: whether required here per verified
      text, and what must be bonded (metallic pipes, drains, structure)
- [ ] No switches/junctions where the verified text forbids them

Underfloor heating in wet rooms additionally: ДБН В.2.5-24:2012 (electric
cable heating systems) applies — its text is not in local references unless
added; mark those checks UNRESOLVED with the standard named.
